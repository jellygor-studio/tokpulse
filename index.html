Frequently Asked Questions

Will TokPulse break when Antigravity updates?

No. Unlike legacy debugger-port injectors that break after every auto-update, TokPulse is built exclusively on official MCP Apps, non-blocking lifecycle hooks, and standard local transcript logs.

Does TokPulse upload my code or chat history?

Never. Free mode makes zero network requests. Pro activation sends only your Polar license key and a one-way SHA-256 device hash to verify your seat limit.

How do I move my Pro license to a new Mac or PC?

Open the Pro modal inside TokPulse and click Deactivate. Your seat is immediately released on Polar so you can activate another machine.

Are the USD cost numbers my actual subscription invoice?

They are reference API-rate equivalents calculated from exact local token counts so you can compare project efficiency and see how much prompt caching saves you.
